# Battlefield agency — 5.3.0

The earlier nonlinear foundation is preserved: five stateful material shells, thirteen shared order-dependent reactions, relic modifiers and six reversible supply approaches. Inspection found that shells were disabled before chapter two and opening wild creatures often died in one ordinary strike. This push makes those early encounters expose that foundation and adds control over shared equipment.

A finite pool of loose tools is drawn from nearby mapped workstations and habitat salvage. Whole furnishings remain scenery. Six tool types—braces, salvage hooks, signal bells, water vessels, grounding coils and binding reeds—have actual owners and one or two uses. A wild formation begins with at most half its members carrying tools. Unarmed creatures spend a telegraphed turn reaching for a nearby remaining object. Ownership cannot duplicate across rivals, reinforcements or saves.

The Field command uses the existing battle picker. Each successful action spends one ready action; range failures spend nothing. Disarm cancels the selected creature’s charge, briefly exposes it and drops its exact remaining tool. Either side can claim that object. Braces become directional one-use cover against aimed attacks, while bursts and nearby contact bypass it. Bells cancel alarms or delay charges, coils drain readiness, hooks punish exposed windups, reeds slow the next action, and jars extinguish burning and supply water to existing reactions. Carried tools, loose tools and cover are rendered directly. No extra inspection popup is added. Live choices remain visible during readiness recovery and explain range or resource restrictions.

Material shell allocation now permits early wild encounters while tutorials, friendly spars and canonical bosses retain their policy. Early creature HP has a modest floor of64, with the existing low early power and no reinforcement budget. Guard interception now requires proximity to the protected creature. This is a limited spatial rule layer, not a rigid-body simulation; ordinary RPG attacks still do not require a melee-range approach.

Snapshots include the shared field, remaining uses, covers and actor ownership. Validation rejects orphan gear, duplicate owners and inconsistent tools before replacing the active world. Tool attacks spend one use per intent, including misses or multi-target hits, and damage-over-time or secondary arcs cannot spend additional uses. Battle gear is local to that encounter and does not become duplicated inventory loot.

Validation: final engine suite398/398passed. Fifteen dedicated battlefield tests and seven integration tests cover disarm/reclaim order, competing pickup, cover geometry, attack alterations, real all-seven-character opening wins with tools and shells, finite use across misses/counters/multi-hit attacks, snapshot restoration and malformed ownership. After the suite, one UI-only correction lets unready characters inspect the same live field choices; syntax and browser verification passed. Cloud browser imported an ordinary validated encounter fixture, disarmed a water-vessel carrier, reclaimed the same vessel as Iona, and showed the creature reaching for a different loose tool. Evidence: verification/battlefield-reclaimed-tool.jpg. No physical-phone performance matrix was completed in this push.

The next major encounter-design step remains objective-based victories: keep a patient safe, stabilize a regulator, prevent theft or disable a signal. These objectives are a proposed next layer, not implemented by this patch. The current win condition remains defeat every opponent.
