# 5.38 · The listening headland

Veyr’s two remembered-life observatory scenes now use a compact authored headland rather than the inherited maze and scattered service props. The collision floor changes with the architecture: a bridge and gate lead past a copper-roof lodge to one receiver mast, then a spare-vane bay and reply court. The same native origin actions, rewards and choices remain authoritative.

The receiver, ground conductor, vane/coil repair, creature diversion and reply lamps change visibly in the scene. A single field HUD cluster keeps native Use, Run and Crew controls near the movement pad; Characters and equipment remain available through Pause. Camera framing includes both current choices and the creature during its active diversion stage. Saved zoom and Reset view retain their existing owners.

Old Veyr positions, derived Atlas maps and battle topology migrate to the compact floor without discarding earned resources or encounter state. Marked snapshots with impossible actor positions or mismatched engagement floors are rejected. Other 165 scenes retain their finite affordance IDs; the observatory uses its native mechanisms and no generated floor blockers.
