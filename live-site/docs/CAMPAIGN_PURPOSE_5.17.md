# The answering city — release 5.17

## Driving motivation

**Bring the stolen voices home, and make it possible for the city to live without continuing to steal them.**

The campaign now carries that purpose through local tasks, the selected traveller's history, working machinery, community consequences, the last encounter, and the return home. The central discovery is that Clay and Water once allowed an answer to travel back. Removing that return made households into an audience for somebody else's command. Reopening it gives the practical work and the personal story the same direction.

This release develops the existing campaign. It does not make ordinary Mako into captive souls, resurrect Sable's mother, identify Mara with an invented family role, or equate Veyr's living rescue with his recorded daughter. The Wayglass remains an inspectable finite tool; the fictional network does not claim to establish a mathematical theorem.

## A purpose through all three acts

`journeyPurpose(state)` provides a short intention and a specific causal reason for every chapter, origins, the homecoming and the completed journey. The field HUD keeps the intention near the current task. **Clues & destinations** contains the chapter's reason, the selected traveller's promise, walking destinations and the actual help the city has earned. Pause also connects the intention with the next task.

The remembered journey lead remains the narrative lead after a combat-party swap. Legacy saves fall back through the known origin, chronicle hero and party. Purpose text is read-only: it awards no progress and chooses no civic answer.

## The city helps in the final encounter

Community contributions come from actual completed works and activated services. Up to three real sources are credited for each kind of help. The guidance panel names those places and states the effect explicitly.

| Kind of help | Bounded effect against the Choir |
| --- | --- |
| Return | The first warning gains 1.2 seconds. Multiple repaired returns share that one pause. |
| Care | Each crew member's first direct Choir hit is halved. |
| Witness | The first correctly completed two-voice interruption opens the Choir for five seconds instead of three. |
| Commons | The first Guard gives every living crew member six additional MP and eases eight strain. |

These are separate tactical consequences, not a combined virtue score. The crew can enter and win without any optional contribution. The existing requirement for different crew members to answer differently remains intact.

Uses are serialized with the battle, so importing or resuming cannot replenish a spent contribution. Invalid optional context fails save preflight before replacing the live journey. Ordinary and older battles remain valid without this context.

One narrower link now joins creature care and trade: a qualifying peaceful passage can protect already-carried cargo on that district's route once. It requires the creatures to remain alive and withdraw, actual care or trust, no crew-inflicted harm or stolen tool, and settled creature-memory evidence. It creates no body drops or free cargo, and an old completed encounter cannot protect a shipment accepted afterward.

See [CITY_ANSWER_SYSTEM.md](CITY_ANSWER_SYSTEM.md) for exact qualifying sources and persistence rules.

## Home remains part of the journey

Optional visits change after the first rescue, the archive's copied dates, the Crown's defeat, Sable's departure from the uniform, and the discovery of the original recording. Brief repeat lines follow each first conversation. Visits use existing journal entries and give no mandatory reward or gate.

The final conversation first acknowledges the city and the selected lead's work. Sable's choice about his mother's recording remains a separate personal choice: keep it for the family, or share it publicly once. Community help does not grant consent to use that recording. Both choices return to Mara's **“You came home.”** The selected traveller also receives a fitting continuation, with room at the table for the ensemble.

New prose uses the existing subtitle and dialogue presentation. This release does not claim new recorded performances or regenerated voice assets.

## Hearing the return

Each completed work has a distinct, short motif through the existing Clay and Water instrument. Completion gives a clear reply; returning to that repaired place permits one quieter answer. Unfinished work does not sound like an earned response.

The synthesis is finite and bounded: at most five phrases of three notes, no looping layer and no accumulating feedback. The controller cancels on pause, scene or journey change and waits while dialogue or another full-screen activity is active. It observes the music-volume preference, including mute. The generated tones are a digital model, not measured hydrophone data or newly recorded character speech.
