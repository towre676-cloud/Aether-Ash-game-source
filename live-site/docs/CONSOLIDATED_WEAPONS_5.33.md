# 5.33 — The weapon atelier

The phone layout keeps Weapon, Character, Inventory and Inspect as separate accessible tabs. Existing inventory and character tools remain available.

Equip, temper, bind, extract and attune use the existing campaign inventory and transaction engine. Every offer shows its exact stat changes, losses, material price and available resources. A replacement attunement explicitly shows the stat it removes. Unaffordable offers explain the shortage and disable spending. Single-use offers reject stale equipment, changed bearers, restored journeys and battle state. Valid legacy equipment remains compatible; malformed negative growth ranks and resources are rejected.

The first newly earned hostile equipment reward now includes a weapon at the same level, rarity, power, value and reward count. It replaces the first artifact within the existing reward budget rather than adding an extra reward. Existing claims and recovered equipment remain unchanged, and discovery history prevents repeated first-weapon awards after salvage.
