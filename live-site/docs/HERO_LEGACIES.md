# Earned body histories — 5.5.0

All seven playable leads can earn each of three optional body histories. They are sidegrades with new verbs and visible consequences, not another permanent stat multiplier. An actual settled regulator or supply objective, or a repair, earns the kiln-grown arm. Escort/signal objectives and successful rescue, feeding or soothing earns the reed-tuned ear. Knowledge discoveries, scouting and successful diversions earn the ash-bound shadow. Failed, unknown and repeated source events cannot earn another deed. Each hero retains at most32 deeds; the shared settlement index retains128 source keys. A signature attack can be recorded alongside a real deed.

The existing character entry screen shows the latest deed, remembered signature, current appearance, unlocked forms and each tradeoff. Attuning the first body history is free. A later attunement costs one Ether; leaving a form is free. All changes require field mode. Earned forms stay unlocked, and the roster's existing new-telling action carries the selected hero's actual history. Local drop-in copies earned identity without importing another journey's levels, supplies, money or campaign flags. Front and back portrait textures receive restrained ceramic, reed or ash additions on the existing relief; these are artwork overlays, not a new skeletal model. The same transformations have an exported in-world canvas overlay.

In combat, each equipped body history can be used twice per encounter, always consuming a ready action and real MP. The kiln-grown arm spends5MP to anchor a crossing for12seconds. One aimed attack actually crossing its point is intercepted; bursts and close contact bypass it. The hand cannot pick up another field tool while anchoring, and a carried tool must be dropped before beginning. The ceramic form also reduces ordinary physical outgoing damage by10%. The reed-tuned ear spends5MP to extend a charged threat by0.8seconds, only once per creature; electrical and explicitly sonic hits deal15% extra incoming damage. The ash-bound shadow spends7MP to move one adjacent floor tile, chooses only known reachable unoccupied floor, leaves five seconds of exposure, and retains one echo for an attack already aimed at the old location. It cannot pass through walls or invent movement topology.

`hero-legacy.js` owns persistent normalization/validation, event settlement, attunement, summary and drop-in copy, and the bounded `b.legacy` runtime. Runtime snapshots preserve uses, read creatures, anchors and shadows. The root integration supplies `context.heroLegacies`, saves/restores `b.legacy`, calls the advance/interception/damage/tool-hand hooks, and carries persistent history through convergence/new tellings. This is an original implementation informed by the character-defining transformations and returning legacy roster described on [Wildermyth's developer page](https://wildermyth.itch.io/wildermyth).

Validation:13 focused engine tests cover all seven heroes earning all forms; no-reward losses/repetition; paid attunement and field-only changes; bounded/rejected save state; exact companion history without wealth/level imports; real crossing/expiry/tool-hand behavior; charged-threat reading; legal ash movement and one-use stale-aim interception; runtime roundtrips; resource failure; and the physical tradeoff. Existing roster and portrait tests also pass, including shader-failure fallback with form changes. Browser/mobile visual review belongs to the combined build validation.
