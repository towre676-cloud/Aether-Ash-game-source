# 5.35 · The field forge

Armory now opens as one equipment destination at a time across desktop and phone. The weapon occupies a full illustrated forge surface, with the current growth offer beside it. Costs and exact stat changes precede the paid action. Return, resources and destination navigation remain outside the content scroller. Weapon properties and the owned rack disclose on demand; Satchel workstations share one folded work surface.

Seven native vector weapon forms now have separate steel, bronze, timber, leather, crystal and engraving details. The Bearer uses the selected traveller’s original front/back artwork, with a renderer created only while that view is active and destroyed when leaving or closing. The prototype knight is hidden.

Pause is a parchment field journal. The adventure HUD uses floating captions, thin vitality strokes and original crew miniatures. Native D-pad, face controls, zoom recovery, movement, inventory, quotes, saves and music preferences keep their existing ownership.

94 focused tests pass. Browser checks cover desktop and phone forge transactions, Satchel inspection, native bearer identity and teardown, journal and crew readability, ordinary Veyr play, and four viewport sizes. In the 390×844 phone check, the long-name weapon’s cost and upgrade button are both above the persistent dock with no horizontal overflow. A compact-landscape crew/route overlap was found and repaired.

Evidence is in `verification/field-interface-5.35.json` and its linked screenshots/log. Screenshots show isolated practice equipment. The browser uses software 3D fallback; this release does not claim an Android hardware performance result.
