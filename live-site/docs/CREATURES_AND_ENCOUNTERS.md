# Creatures and early encounters — 5.2.0

The live game now has 432 catalog records from 48 delivered creature silhouettes: 144 existing native, elder and regional records, plus 288 tactical adaptations. These are varieties sharing family artwork, not 432 newly illustrated bodies. Adaptations change role, element, status, telegraph shape, AI trait, stats, palette and salvage. Twelve habitat pools include opening-tier creatures and broaden through the early and middle campaign. Recent species are excluded when alternatives exist.

Every origin lead begins with practical stats, an equipped origin core and Focus, one usable socket, MP and supplies. Keeper dialogue preserves earned progression. The worksite can be visited immediately and returned from before completing story choices. Sable’s home remains safe. Mira now uses actual archive wings. Older past saves receive missing combat kit without resetting progress, and invalid saved positions recover to walkable floor.

Random encounters count actual collision-resolved ground distance. First contact occurs after 5–9 eligible tiles. Further intervals are 18–32 outdoors and 24–40 in selected infested interiors. Door, NPC and camp bubbles do not accumulate encounter debt; idle time, blocked movement, menus, dialogue, battle and teleports do not count. Post-fight travel grace is six tiles, retreat ten, and doors receive transition protection. Homes, clinics, inns and sanctuaries remain safe. A visible territory label and a nearby-creature warning explain the risk.

Random fights have a dedicated settlement path: XP, active-party mastery, credits, actual species drops and journal sightings/victories; they never complete chapters, set objective flags, clear a story threat or grant guardian equipment. Tier-one fights are solo creatures, with reduced power and armour and no alarm reinforcements. Dynamic fights are serialized and validated so saving mid-encounter works. Creatures spawn on reachable floor. A searchable in-game guide shows all varieties, habitat filters, tactics and journey sightings; the standalone guide uses the same catalog.

The encounter model follows established distance-and-region patterns documented in [RPG Maker MZ](https://rpgmakerofficial.com/product/MZ_help-en/01_07_03.html). [Nintendo’s Bravely Second overview](https://www.nintendo.com/en-gb/News/2016/March/9-Things-you-need-to-know-about-Bravely-Second-End-Layer-1090321.html) also documents encounter-rate control. The specific tile budgets above are this game’s tuning choices, not universal values prescribed by those sources.

Running repairs are documented in verification/RUNNING_AUDIT.md: measured source rectangles preserve all 128 poses; crew rear idle/walk and starter rear idle heading are normalized; near-horizontal wall slides preserve the rear view; gait scales and extended-foot floor contacts stay consistent.

Validation: the final engine suite passed 375/375. A separate 20-check encounter/save run and 15-check animation run passed. Coverage includes seven starting heroes versus all four monster roles, catalog behavior, actual reward and story isolation, dynamic fight save/restore, every origin route and reachable spawn positions. Browser QA started a new Iona journey, walked into a random fight in the opening interior before the keeper objective, and verified the 432-entry searchable guide and its sighting count.
