# 5.39 · The working observatory

Veyr’s two observatory scenes receive a denser artistic treatment. Limestone courts and wall courses, weathered copper, and fractured slate replace the broad matte fills. A bounded canvas atlas supplies the same authored material families to the existing GPU and software renderers without adding an image download.

The lodge has stone trim, receiver faces, gutters, pipework, ventilators and a wind pennant. The mast has cabinets, gauges, insulators and calibration detail. Coping, cliff strata, a large inlaid wind chart, and a small tidal reply station give the court and its surroundings a designed composition. Real mechanism positions have flush service plates and visible grounding sockets. New solids remain off the native walk mask.

Phone framing uses actual silhouettes and current interaction points, including both cable endpoints. It retains the east-side sightline past the mast, the selected manual lens and exact inverse floor picking. Landscape uses the clear field between the side controls. Exposed cliff geometry uses physical depth bands above the sea; buried foundations in other scenes keep their existing rendering behavior.
