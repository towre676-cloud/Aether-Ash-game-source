# Objective encounters and practical instruments — 5.4.0

Wild encounters now rotate between protecting an escort, stabilizing pressure, securing supplies and shutting down a hostile signal. Every lead receives an objective on their first wild encounter; every fourth later contact remains an ordinary fight. Story guardians and authored campaign milestones retain their own contracts. All four goals can finish with opponents still alive. Clearing the threat is also a valid way to save an intact objective; losing the protected asset, the supplies or the time window causes withdrawal without advancing story flags.

Escorts require an active guide action, company within three tiles, and a reachable floor route. A paid repeat guide action can find a real detour around blocking creatures; narrow blocked corridors do not become passable. Regulators require control adjustments followed by safe pressure stability. Supplies are secured in paid stages while scavengers can steal them back. Signal shutdown stages can be reconnected by sabotage. Enemy approaches and attacks are telegraphed, follow local floor topology and cannot sabotage through walls. The protected actor can receive Mend, Ward and healing supplies.

Hydrophone listening reads live threats and pressure and can delay an unread charge once. For mechanisms it calibrates a pressure release (45 rather than30 reduction) or a reply window that protects one shutdown stage from reconnection. Clay records a frozen family/kind/shape rhythm; a bounded replay emits a decoy that redirects one matching current or future aimed attack. Recorded patterns persist across encounters, origin convergence and new tellings, capped at eight patterns. Directional Wayglass lines intercept only actual crossing aimed attacks, with finite charges, range checks and counter openings. This is a geometric game mechanic; it does not claim to run a Kakeya optimization theorem during combat. Listening, recording and replay also sound bounded phrases through the existing digital Dyletsky string/water path, with recorded shape and windup affecting the phrase.

Timeline repair choices, civic destinies, regional records and physical restorations alter actual asset approaches, escort routes, control counts, time windows, integrity and available shared tools. Successful local encounters leave nonstacking shelter routes, bypasses, reply windows or spare braces for later fights in the same location. The full outcome account is bounded to128 records/local aids. Nonlethal objective completions add16XP/16credits plus one goal-specific supply; clear-all objective completions add8/8 plus that supply. Base encounter rewards remain. Only actually defeated species contribute monster defeats and species drops. No creature deaths or loot are invented for a rescue victory.

Goals, clocks, progress and integrity stay in a compact HUD. Actions, live analysis, retained patterns and angle selection share the existing picker with persistent Back/Cancel. No new inspection modal is introduced. Keyboard/controller picker traversal includes native pattern/angle selections. Missing legacy objective/instrument state remains valid; invalid new geometry, ownership or retained memory is rejected before replacing an active save. Import references are versioned through the changed dependency graph so returning browsers receive the matching runtime.
