# Chapel of Rain playtest

Release 5.16 connects a complete investigation, physical repair, guardian response,
reward and return visit. The intended session length is **20–30 minutes**. This is
a design target; it has not been established by independent human playtests.

## Start a temporary session

Open the game's **Developer portal**, select **04 Chapel of Rain**, then choose
**Play the Chapel investigation**. The practice crew has a finite prepared kit.
This session does not write ordinary journey saves or traveller slots.

The dedicated entry tracks the optional return-channel investigation from the
start. **Explore this chapter** uses the ordinary campaign objective instead;
**Test the guardian** starts at the prepared guardian checkpoint.

Use the objective button to walk the current route. **Clues & destinations** can
walk to a particular person, tool or mechanism. Ask for progressively stronger
hints when needed. **Full assistance** also follows the remembered campaign
musical phrases; it does not choose story decisions or battle actions.

## What to observe

Ask the player to speak their thoughts. Let them choose their preferred guidance
mode. Record where they hesitate before explaining the intended solution.

| Part of the session | Question to resolve |
| --- | --- |
| Entry and first destination | Can the player describe the immediate problem and start moving without searching settings? |
| Soot, plate and two accounts | Do the physical marks and witnesses explain why the return matters? |
| Feed shutter and lower drain | Does the player understand the order of operations from the world's feedback? |
| Vestry or restored answering bell | Is the alternative route discoverable? Does the silence in the vestry phrase make sense? |
| Fitting and testing | Can the player distinguish the outgoing pulse from its answer? |
| Shelter or lure | Can the player predict the two preparations' different consequences? |
| Ash Herald | Is the initial toll distinguishable from the returning ring? Can the player explain what Break and Guard each do? |
| Inherited equipment | Does the Rainbell item suggest a new action rather than only a larger number? |
| Return visit | Does opening the dry shelter or witness stair feel like a consequence of this player's work? |
| Art, animation and controls | Are figures and labels readable, action poses clear, and commands reachable in the player's viewport? |

## Local observations

Open **Playtest notes** in the temporary session badge. Mark **I'm lost**,
**That felt good**, or **Something broke** at the relevant moment. **Export
observations** downloads a JSON report with elapsed time, selected fixture,
bounded interaction events, hint stages, assistance choices, scene transitions,
battle events, pauses, errors and markers. It does not send a report anywhere.
Reports contain no journey save, personal identity, free-text transcript or
browser credentials. The maximum retained history is 2,000 events; the report
states how many earlier events were dropped if the limit is reached.

Elapsed seconds include pauses. Use the pause events and the observer's notes
when interpreting duration. A browser automation session is useful for checking
controls; it is not evidence of a new player's comprehension or enjoyment.

## After the guardian

On the journey prompt, choose **Return to the repaired bell circuit**. The guide
can take the crew to the crypt diverter. Finish the aftercare decision, then
inspect the persistent service: the dry shelter and stair, or the witness's route
to the archive. Repeating the service must not grant another initial supply.

## Debrief

Ask the player to explain the return-channel problem, their preparation choice,
and their response to the guardian without reading the instructions back to
them. Ask which moment they most wanted to continue and which moment most made
them want to stop. Record the player's words separately from the observer's
interpretation.

Use the first sessions to revise guidance and pacing before adding more content.
Compare the same sequence in ordinary destination guidance and Full assistance,
and in a desktop view and the player's actual phone when available. The supplied
responsive harness checks browser layout only; it does not emulate a phone's
hardware, input latency or graphics driver.
