checkpoint. The campaign commit remains an ancestor, rather than being
recreated from selected files. Its answering network, two-way replies, repairs,
homecomings, selected-lead purpose, peaceful shipments and earned finale

## Living vegetation research

Two contemporary studies were located and read:

- [Living Meadow Studio](https://chatgpt.com/library?file_id=libfile_b0bc45d7311881919efd31ab6b997e33&account_id=personal): a Three.js 0.180.0 demonstration that imports separate grass renderer and ecology modules, with biome, wind, rain, sunlight and traveller controls.
- [Offline vegetation preview](https://chatgpt.com/library?file_id=libfile_768ae8b6f24c8191a15d6d8e35d0a098&account_id=personal): a dependency-free Canvas study with ten habitat choices. Its own notice identifies it as an artistic inspection preview, separate from the deployed game and the Three.js renderer.

Those studies inform the eighth next-art brief. Their research interfaces are
not substituted for the current game's production Canvas renderer, and their
ecology or save behavior is not claimed as integrated. The next implementation
needs a renderer adapter, habitat/palette alignment, travel clearances, bounded
animation and separate ecology/save validation. Exact study references are
retained in `development/art/NEXT-ART-BRIEFS.md`.
