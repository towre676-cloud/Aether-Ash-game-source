# Living world and playable histories — 5.5.0

The existing seven-character journey now uses the same materials and encounter rules during exploration and combat. The expansion adds sixteen creature behavior profiles, sixteen functional anatomy profiles, twelve environmental repair and listening profiles, and three optional body histories. These are mechanics for the existing campaign, origins and frontiers. They do not require another campaign selector or a separate minigame.

## Creatures pursue readable goals

Wild species receive hungry, nesting, territorial, scavenging or frightened motives through their creature family. Relationship tables distinguish prey, threats and friends. Plans alternate these motives with the existing objective and ordinary attack plans, so ecology does not erase the battle. A predator can attack a nearby prey species; a wounded friendly creature can receive protection; a scavenger can seek a real remaining tool in the shared pool and carry it toward a den. Movement follows checked four-way floor routes. Breaking a locomotion part slows these steps and can prevent physical escape.

Nourishment spends an appropriate Tonic or Ether. A calming rhythm costs four MP; a sounding lure costs three MP and uses a reachable marked angle. Rescue spends a Moss wrap or Tonic on a trapped, afflicted or seriously wounded creature. Securing a nesting shelter spends a carried brace or reed use. Every successful interaction consumes one ready action, while invalid range, missing supplies and already completed actions consume nothing. Treatment and shelter can end hostility with the animal still alive. Attacking an animal creates a grudge and blocks an immediate friendly release. These rules make nonlethal actions a real choice with costs and consequences.

Individual memories record trust, grudges, meetings, rescue, nourishment, den and stolen tool type. Identity is stable across encounter serials in the same place and family, while an encounter ID prevents duplicate settlement. Memory is capped at 128 individuals and 128 settled encounters. Escaping with a tool spends that finite local object rather than cloning equipment into an inventory. Living creatures that withdraw leave the active enemy set, cease blocking an escort, and receive neither a defeat entry nor species drops. Retained regional relationships survive the origin-to-present transition. A new telling retains learned rules and character histories while beginning a fresh physical regional situation.

## Anatomy changes available actions

Functional parts are separate from the five existing borrowed material shells. Eligible wild creatures have three named parts drawn from sixteen anatomy profiles. The existing action picker can target one large, readable part at a time. A precise hit damages ordinary HP through the normal armor, shield and reaction path and also wears the selected part. It costs one ready action and requires a clear, close floor approach.

Breaking a sound-producing organ interrupts reinforcement calls; a horn narrows a sweeping charge into a weaker single-target attack; locomotion damage limits movement and escape. Other parts govern guard rebuilding, heat, repair hymns, focus siphoning, root choruses, condition shedding and charged crests. A break alters an already charged intent as well as future plans. At most one salvageable part per creature becomes a uniquely identified finite object in the shared tool pool. A detached plate can become a brace and a claw can become a hook. Repeated hits, recovery attempts and save restoration cannot manufacture the same part twice. The shared-pool validation budget accommodates original tools and detached parts.

## Position has dependable physical consequences

Shove and draw-closer actions use the actual two-dimensional vector between actors. Displacement samples a route through known floor, stops at walls and other actors, and previews its endpoint. A legal action consumes readiness; an action with no legal movement leaves readiness untouched. Both participants in a collision receive damage through the ordinary battle resolver. Moving a charging attacker changes the origin of its committed aim rather than silently granting a fresh aim. A redirected line attack, or one involving a deliberately repositioned creature, can strike another enemy along the real line. Bosses retain their anchored behavior, and ordinary burst/contact attacks retain their authored target policy.

## One travel kit serves several purposes

Brace planks, salvage hooks, signal bells, water vessels, grounding coils and binding reeds can be gathered as finite marked spares. Two-part assemblies, one-tool alternatives and hand-played rhythms solve the same environmental situation in different ways. The twelve profiles cover sluices, catwalks, indexing gates, wind relays, burrowed trails, rootwalks, silt grates, cellar landings, echo galleries, rime channels, witness arches and ground loops. Habitat and authored origin identity choose an appropriate profile.

Placement reserves story entrances, actors and furniture footprints. A closed optional repair tile is only chosen when all other original floor remains connected around it. The actual walking mask and click-to-travel pathfinder both respect its closed state. Repairing it opens that floor and improves local encounter conditions, with a one-time eight-credit and one-supply reward. Sable's small home remains a quiet room with spare tools; his first worksite provides the repair and listening situation. Each of the seven opening journeys has a usable site within its two existing backdrops.

A carried spare can instead be packed for the next local encounter. Packing removes it from the travel kit, adds exactly one ordinary shared battlefield object, and permits either side to claim it. Constructing the same encounter context twice preserves the same reservation; the following encounter cannot receive another copy. Retreat does not refund the transferred object. The field kit holds at most thirty of each tool type. Source, site, plan and outcome ledgers are bounded and validated before a save replaces active progress.

## Preparation and knowledge change the journey

A local listening point gives partial evidence such as scraping, pressure returns or a conductor answering itself. A quiet approach uses the gap between calls; a hook can prepare a flank; a bell and reeds can prepare a distant lure. Preparation changes the next real formation's checked starting positions, delays its first charge, suppresses a reinforcement opportunity or extends an objective window as appropriate. Each room supplies one finite prepared approach. The same encounter retains its preparation through restoration; a later contact does not repeatedly receive it.

Mechanisms have actual three-step low, middle and high return sequences. Tapping the correct sequence works immediately even without discovering its clue. Listening records the sequence, and solving it by knowledge writes a different character deed from repairing it with materials. This allows an experienced player to use learned rules early on a reroll. Knowledge persists into a new telling; physical repair completion, gathered spares and prepared approaches begin fresh. A wrong note resets or starts a new matching prefix without consuming a quest flag or inventing a combat victory.

## A character card contains a playable history

Successful objective, ecology, repair, scouting and knowledge actions can write a deed for the hero who performed them. Source IDs prevent repeated settlement from earning repeated histories. Every playable lead can earn the same three optional forms. Each card retains the recent deed, earned forms, equipped appearance and a signature remembered pattern. Local party joining carries earned identity into the receiving journey, with that journey's supplies and progression.

The kiln-grown arm anchors a one-use crossing without consuming a brace. It occupies the carried-tool hand while holding that crossing and reduces ordinary physical damage by ten percent. The reed-tuned ear delays a charged threat once per creature but takes fifteen percent more electrical or sonic damage. The ash-bound shadow steps to verified adjacent floor and leaves a one-use silhouette against an attack already aimed at the old position, while exposing the user briefly. These verbs cost readiness and MP and have a two-use encounter limit. The first earned attunement is free, subsequent changes cost one Ether, and removing a form is free between encounters. Bodies and existing roster presentations receive visible additions rather than hidden stat labels.

## Integration and evidence

`living-world.js` constructs the common encounter context and settles ecology, preparation and earned deeds before the encounter is released. `battle.js` dispatches the paid commands, applies anatomy and preparation to enemy intents, handles nonlethal withdrawal, and snapshots ecology, anatomy and legacy runtime state. `world.js`, `origins.js` and save preflight preserve the appropriate current-life and retained-history state. Old saves with no new state remain supported; malformed geometry, body histories, anatomy and creature state are rejected before replacing the active world.

The independent `living-world-integration.test.mjs` exercises actual World and Battle objects: all seven opening contexts; paid rescue and duplicate settlement; a broken sound organ changing an already charged alarm; unique detached material across snapshots; wall-aware two-axis displacement and enemy collateral; nonlethal loot accounting; the ceramic crossing and occupied hand; save, convergence and new-telling continuity; malformed saves; finite tool assemblies that open real navigation floor; prepared formation and first-charge changes; a knowledge solution without clue flags; one transferred travel tool; and correct attribution of a companion's nourishment deed. Dedicated modules additionally test their profile coverage, finite costs, geometry, relationship changes and validators.

This is a bounded active-room simulation with authored discrete material and knowledge rules. It does not claim a continuously simulated whole-world ecosystem, a freeform rigid-body physics engine, fully volumetric character rigs or network multiplayer. Physical Android/controller hardware coverage remains a separate verification limit.

The design sources were the developers' explanations of [Rain World's living ecosystem](https://blog.playstation.com/2017/01/16/rain-world-surviving-in-a-living-ecosystem/), [Rain World's relationship and AI modules](https://unity.com/blog/exploring-procedural-design-rain-world), [Monster Hunter Wilds' focused wounds and breakable parts](https://news.xbox.com/en-us/2025/02/28/monster-hunter-wilds-tips-from-developers/), [Nintendo's consistent object affordances](https://www.nintendo.com/us/whatsnew/ask-the-developer-vol-9-the-legend-of-zelda-tears-of-the-kingdom-part-5/), [Larian's exploration and improvised actions](https://baldursgate3.game/news/community-update-15-absolute-frenzy_49), [Subset's manipulation and interface constraints](https://media.gdcvault.com/gdc2019/presentations/Into%20the%20Breach%20Postmortem%20Final.pdf), [Mobius's curiosity and environmental pathing](https://www.mobiusdigitalgames.com/news/the-intentionality-of-wandering), and [Wildermyth's playable transformations and legacies](https://wildermyth.itch.io/wildermyth). All specific names, encounters, material rules and character histories described here are adaptations for Ash & Aether.


## Release verification

The final complete engine suite passed 525/525 tests (116.7 seconds, two test workers). The release also passes module import, syntax and whitespace checks. This includes 21 creature behavior tests, 19 anatomy/position tests, 13 world-affordance tests, 13 hero-legacy tests, six UI/render tests and 16 actual integration tests. An earlier full run found an old-save compatibility issue from adding a null visual field to unchanged heroes; the runtime now preserves legacy saves without that mutation. An extra integration check confirms a finite ceramic crossing intercepts actual regulator sabotage once. All changed dependency references use 5.5.0, including transitive imports, so returning browsers receive consistent modules.

Cloud-browser verification resumed Rook's saved opening workshop, used the actual contextual floor-listening picker, revealed hydrophone evidence, prepared the quiet approach, returned to play, opened the traveller roster, earned the ash-bound shadow from that deed, and attuned it for free. The actual character portrait and field sprite show its ash detail. The shared picker reopened and returned to the world without an inspection dialog. Evidence: verification/living-legacy-release.jpg. The browser connection was temporarily unavailable during testing and recovered. A physical Android device/controller matrix was not run.
