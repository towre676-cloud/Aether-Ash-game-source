THE ARMORY'S RULES

EVERY CHOICE CHANGES THE WORLD

Tap an item to inspect it. Double-tap to equip it, or use Equip in its inspection card. Drag equipment across satchel cells or into equipment slots. Tap equipped gear to compare and remove it. Use the vault for overflow and safekeeping.

Hunt: defeat a creature to see a deterministic, encounter-specific drop. The world region, creature type, difficulty and level alter the reward. The same encounter ID can never pay twice.

Runes: twelve socketable sigils permanently alter stats and environmental harmonics; extraction is possible at a material cost. Save up to three build presets and switch them without losing displaced items.

Foundry: create deterministic regional equipment with exact costs. The hunter’s codex records defeated enemies and reveals anatomy-driven slot preferences.

Forge: salvage spare items for scrap, essence and rare starshards. Temper the selected item up to +8. Protect treasures with the lock button.

Battle: gear affects damage, material shielding, elemental resonance, resistance and terrain grip in a nonlinear numerical preview. It does not replace the live game's canonical encounter engine.

Offline integration prototype v2. The published Ash & Aether game has not been modified.
