# A&A 5.28 — 45-minute physical-device playtest

This is the unresolved verification gate that automation cannot replace.

## Setup
- Android phone, normal battery mode, no charger.
- Start from a fresh or copied save at chapter 1; do not use the dev portal for the first 20 minutes.
- Use normal touchscreen movement, pinch zoom, Pause, Mako and Gear.
- Record only local observations; no screen recording is required.

## Minutes 0–10 — control comfort
Check accidental movement after pinch, thumb reach to the combat row, label obstruction at 100/150/200% zoom, target selection, and whether the new context UI keeps important actions visible.

## Minutes 10–20 — encounter legibility
Play at least three optional encounters. For each, write one sentence answering: "What made this enemy different before I opened a menu?" If the answer is only element/color/HP, identity failed.

## Minutes 20–30 — reward transformation
Equip or choose at least one verb-changing reward. The next encounter should provide a reason to try it. Record whether the new verb was used voluntarily before the game explicitly prompted it.

## Minutes 30–38 — unguided exploration
Ignore the guided objective and follow a track, sound, unusual plant pattern, geometry clue or NPC routine. Record whether curiosity produced anything meaningful within eight minutes.

## Minutes 38–45 — boss or side activity
Use the dev portal only now if needed to reach a guardian. Confirm that a phase changes arena rules or available movement, not just damage/HP. Alternatively play a side activity and ask whether it is enjoyable without its reward.

## Device checks at minute 45
- Heat: cool / warm / uncomfortable.
- Battery drop.
- Any sustained frame stutter.
- Touch targets missed more than twice.
- Pinch zoom ever mistaken for movement/attack.
- Text that required zooming the browser/UI itself.

## Exit question
Would you voluntarily keep playing another 15 minutes if no new story scene were promised? Why or why not?
