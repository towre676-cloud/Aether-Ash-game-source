The World is the Encounter

This is an offline interactive QA map, not the released game or the Three.js cinematic viewer. It exercises the same real v8 numerical game logic. A creature is fightable only from its own physical position.

Tap an animal to target it. Amber ring = selected creature · pale ring = party · red mark = blocked crossing. Your footsteps leave disturbances and change wildlife.
