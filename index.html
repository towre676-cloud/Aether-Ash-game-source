The Living World laboratory

A real, archived standalone HTML experiment from A&A's vegetation and ecology development. Walk forward, trample grass, harvest samples, change fire and moisture, advance the clock, and export or import a local save.

This lab is a development artifact, not the complete live game. It is preserved here so visitors can interact with part of the work without signing in, installing dependencies or relying on an external server.
