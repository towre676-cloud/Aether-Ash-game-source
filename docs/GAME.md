# Ash & Aether — world guide

## The promise

**The city burns its memories. The water remembers yours.** *Clay and Water* frames its fantasy exploration around seven lives and one unfinished song. Its public presentation names four acts:

1. **ASH — The stolen song**
2. **WATER — The undercity well**
3. **AETHER — The sky engine**
4. **CLAY — Orchard after fire**

The live opening also offers character choice, an overture, a field guide, an orchestral section and entry to a 3D dungeon. This document intentionally avoids fabricated plot details and unverified player progression.

## Where to play

The original game is at https://ash-aether-clay-water.towre676.chatgpt.site/. Its save is local to the browser; a new origin/address can require transferring an exported save through the in-game **Pause → Import save** flow. The external host's public access cannot be guaranteed by the repository.

The **Living World reconciliation laboratory** is [here](../labs/living-world.html). It is independent of the main game's campaign state and does not import campaign saves. Use its own **Export save JSON** and **Import save JSON** controls, and make a backup before resetting it.

## Design systems under development

- **Place as gameplay.** Approach, threshold, observation, manipulation, encounter, aftermath and release create a rhythm for authored locations; mechanisms include floodgates, resonance locks, counterweights, wind vanes and memory lenses.
- **Living environments.** Standalone research investigates terrain and grass with growth, harvesting, trampling, fire and water response. The public lab demonstrates some of these interactions directly.
- **Encounters and equipment.** Combat feel, readable encounters, loot and armory expansion have their own prototype and verification work.
- **Sound and presentation.** Adaptive orchestral music is developed in the separate [Hydrophone repository](https://github.com/towre676-cloud/hydrophone); A&A's acoustic-system prototypes explore world events and cues.
- **Camera, touch and accessibility.** Zoom, mobile controls, reduced dead space, clear spatial landmarks and less scrolling are recurring design priorities.

These are mixed *design goals, prototype work and live-game features*. They should not be read as a verified list of everything presently available on the public game site.

## For new visitors

Open the main game, choose a character, and explore. If access to the original Site is restricted, use the standalone lab for an immediate sample of a world system and browse the development material here.
